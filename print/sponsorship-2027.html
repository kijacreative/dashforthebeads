Join us for one of Oak Cliff's most cherished traditions!

The Dash for the Beads and the Go Oak Cliff Mardi Gras Parade come together to create a vibrant celebration. This large community event is all in support of children's health and creativity.

We hope you can be part of the festivities and a worthwhile cause. All net proceeds of the Dash go directly to Oak Cliff organizations helping kids thrive through movement, nutrition, and imagination.

$450,000+raised since 2009 for local schools and non-profits that promote physical fitness, healthy eating, and the arts.

Race & Fun Run

  • 5K Race: a scenic, challenging, chip-timed course through Kessler Park, starting and ending at Davis & Clinton in front of the Kessler Theater.
  • 1-Mile Fun Run: a family-friendly stroll before the 5K, so parents and youth runners can do both.
  • Awards: medals for the top 3 male and female runners in 9 age categories.

Mardi Gras Festival

One of the best Mardi Gras parties in North Texas starts with us! From before the racers take off right up to the start of the parade, enjoy music, food trucks, fresh mimosas, and craft beer. Our festival is a prime viewing spot for the Go Oak Cliff Mardi Gras Parade!

Registration

Registration for the 2027 Dash for the Beads is open now. Don't miss your chance to run, walk, party, and make a difference.
